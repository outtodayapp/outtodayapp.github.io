OutToday

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

OutToday is an app that shows events, places and deals across the United Arab Emirates. We built it to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

No account, no tracking

You can use OutToday without creating an account. The app has no advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal data.

Your location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location on your device to show how far events and places are from you and to sort by distance. The distance is calculated on your phone. Your location is not sent to us and is not stored. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working without it.

Favourites and settings

Items you save as favourites and your filter settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Calendar

When you tap "Add to calendar", the app asks for permission to add that one event to your calendar. It does not read your existing calendar entries.

Data we receive

To show listings, the app downloads them from our database, which is hosted by Supabase. Like any internet service, the hosting provider receives technical data needed to deliver content, such as your IP address and device type, and may keep it in short-term server logs for security. We do not use this data to identify you.

Links to other services

Buttons such as Google Maps, Waze, WhatsApp, phone calls and "Tickets and booking" open other apps or websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave OutToday.

Reporting a problem

If you use "Report a problem", your email app opens a message to us. We receive only what you choose to send, including your email address, and use it only to fix the listing and reply to you.

Suggesting an event

If you use "Suggest an event", the app sends us what you type in the form: the event name, date, place, city, link and notes. Your email address is optional and is used only if we need to ask you about the suggestion. Suggestions are stored in our database (Supabase) and are not shown publicly. We check each one against the official page before anything is added, and we delete suggestions and email addresses within 90 days. To have a suggestion deleted sooner, write to outtodayapp.uae@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

The OutToday app has no user accounts. The only personal data we may hold is what you send through "Suggest an event" or "Report a problem": the text of your message and, if you chose to give it, your email address.

To have it deleted, email outtodayapp.uae@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the email address you used (or the event name you suggested). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. We keep nothing after deletion. Even without a request, suggestions and email addresses are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Favourites and settings are stored only on your device: deleting the app deletes them.

Children

OutToday is intended for adults (21+), the legal drinking age in the UAE. Some listings mention venues and offers that serve alcohol. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we add features that use more data (for example optional accounts), we will update this policy before they are released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: outtodayapp.uae@gmail.com
