Frequently asked

Is OutToday free? Yes. The app is free to use and needs no account.

Where does the information come from? From the official websites of venues and organisers, their ticket sellers, and posters shared with us. We list facts (date, time, venue, price) and link to the official page for booking.

Do you sell tickets? No. Booking happens on the organiser's or ticket seller's own site.
